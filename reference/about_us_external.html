External

Inside or outside the lab, connection is what keeps us strong. They’re the bridge that keeps communication clear, collaboration active, and the LIPIST spirit united.

What Do We Do?

The External Division is responsible for managing and maintaining LIPIST’s relationships with external parties. Our division also prepares and conducts ETAP external training programs for students and the general public. These include events such as ETAP Online and ETAP External Training, where we organize the curriculum collaborating with RnD, coordinate speakers, manage participants, and execute the event.In addition, we run a networking and knowledge sharing program called Hello Alumni, where we host talkshow with alumni. Through this event, our alumni provide insights into professional experiences, industry trends, and career development, while strengthening the connection with LIPIST alumni.
