Social Branding

The Social Branding Division shares LIPIST’s journey through visuals, design, and storytelling. They’re the creative minds behind every post, capturing what happens inside the lab and showing it to the world.

What Do We Do?

The Social Branding division is responsible for maintaining the visual identity of the laboratory. We handle all design and creative aspects, ensuring that every publication reflects a consistent and professional brand image. Our responsibilities include designing Instagram stories and feeds, developing visual concepts for campaigns, and managing the overall aesthetic direction of the lab’s social media. In addition, we are in charge of video editing and producing engaging multimedia content to support various programs and events.
