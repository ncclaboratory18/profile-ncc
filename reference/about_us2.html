Our Lab.

Laboratory of Measuring Instrumentation and Power System Indentification (LIPIST) is a university-based laboratory that provides Electrical Measurement laboratory work related to the Undergraduate Curriculum of Sepuluh Nopember Institute of Technology. LIPIST also provides a scheduled calibration of electrical measurement devices, and provides consulting services for industrial electrical system.

Thesis in LIPIST

Protection Coordination

It's about carefully selecting and configuring protective devices, such as circuit breakers and relays, to swiftly respond to faults or disturbances while minimizing disruptions to the electrical grid

Transient Stability

is the ability of a power system to maintain synchronization and return to its normal condition after experiencing significant disturbances

Arc Flash

is the sudden release of heat and light energy produced by the electric current passing through the air, resembling a flash of lightning

Super Capacitor & Batteries

a type of capacitor capable of storing energy on a large scale with an energy amount nearly equivalent to chemical batteries
