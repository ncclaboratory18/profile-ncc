Research & Development

Our R&D Division is the home of new ideas — where we explore, experiment, and create. From developing tools to improving lab systems, they keep LIPIST moving forward with creativity and precision.

What Do We Do?

The Research and Development division is responsible for ensuring the continuity of research activities at LIPIST, including participating in competitions, participating in lecturers’ researches, and preparing modules for external ETAP training. The Research and Development division is also responsible for managing PKM projects involving ITS students and currently is conducting battery research entitled "Development of an Intelligent Fast Charging System Based on Adaptive Machine Learning to Optimize Power Efficiency in Lithium Iron Phosphate Batteries."
