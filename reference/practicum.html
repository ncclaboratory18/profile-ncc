P1

Voltage
Measurement

The experiment assessed a voltmeter's performance across different electrical conditions. It included tests for accuracy with standard AC voltages, examining the influence of frequency, and effectiveness with irregular AC voltages. Additionally, proper voltmeter installation in a circuit was learned, with distinctions made between its function and that of an ammeter.

P2

LCR
Measurement

This experiment involves measuring resistance, capacitance, and inductance in circuits. Methods like the Wheatstone bridge and volt-ampere meter are used for resistance, while LCR meters measure capacitance and inductance. Different techniques, including Maxwell bridge and comparison bridge, are employed for specific measurements, offering insights into electronic component properties.

P3

POWER
Measurement

This experiment explores power measurements in both single-phase and three-phase electrical systems. It covers the techniques for measuring power in single-phase circuits, which are common in household applications, as well as in three-phase systems, prevalent in industrial settings. Understanding the concepts of balanced and unbalanced loads in three-phase systems is crucial for ensuring efficient power distribution and system stability.

P4

USE OF AN
OSCILLOSCOPE

This experiment teaches students about oscilloscope basics, including its functions, calibration, and usage limits. They learn to use it to observe voltage waveforms, measure parameters like amplitude and frequency, and analyze measurement accuracy using math formulas.
