Internal

Inside or outside the lab, connection is what keeps us strong. The Internal Division brings people together — creating teamwork inside LIPIST and building good relations beyond it.

What Do We Do?

The internal division is part of the organization that manages the harmony of laboratory members and maintains communication between laboratory members. The focus of this division is to hold activities to improve the skills of laboratory members and internal laboratory activities and to ensure that every laboratory member feels comfortable and supported.
