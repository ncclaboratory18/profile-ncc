Social Project

The Social Project Division turns what we learn into something meaningful for the community. Through social initiatives and educational projects, they make sure knowledge from the lab can bring real impact to others.

What Do We Do?

The Social Project division is responsible to develop and refine project proposals and technical concepts, perform equipment sizing and technical feasibility analysis to ensure proper implementation, and manage all administrative documentation and compliance requirements related to community service initiatives. The division’s primary programs operate under the flagship initiatives Lipist Mengabdi including grants and competitions such as Direktorat Riset dan Pengabdian kepada Masyarakat (DRPM), Innovillage, and Pertamina Foundation Muda (PFMuda) as well as Lipist Berbagi, which focuses on direct community outreach and social impact activities.
